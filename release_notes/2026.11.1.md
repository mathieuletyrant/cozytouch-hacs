This release stops radiators behind a CozyBox from showing an air
conditioner's controls, brings back the heating status of boilers and towel
racks, and gets ahead of the Home Assistant 2026.11 deprecations.

### 🔧 Radiators behind a CozyBox

A room heated by an electric radiator was treated as an air conditioner :
its thermostat card offered cool, auto, fan only and dry, a fan speed, and
there were Swing, Quiet mode and cooling setpoint entities that did nothing.
Such a room now offers off and heat only, and those entities are no longer
created. This was seen on Taluha, OROSI 2 and Adélis rooms. A room whose
air conditioner or heat pump is behind the same kind of gateway keeps
everything it had.

### 🌡️ Heating status on boilers and heaters

Boilers, thermostats, towel racks and radiators show their heating status
again by default — the burner or the element — as they did before
September. Rooms and air conditioners keep it hidden, since it never moves
there.

### ⚠️ Assist tools renamed

The schedule and absence tools Home Assistant offers to a voice assistant or
an MCP client are now called `cozytouch__get_schedule`,
`cozytouch__set_away_mode` and so on. If a prompt or a script calls them by
their old name, update it. The rest of the change clears the warnings Home
Assistant 2026.11 logs against this integration, before they turn into
errors in 2027.
