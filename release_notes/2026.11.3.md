This release gives hot water tanks a water heater card, with only the modes
the tank really has.

### 🌡️ A water heater card for your tank

Your tank now appears as a water heater in Home Assistant : one card with
its setpoint, its mode and the water temperature (the tank's middle probe).
It writes the same setpoint and mode as the existing select and number, which
stay for your automations. On a new install, the duplicate "Target Temperature DHW"
slider is off by default. Only a Lineo (model 1959) was checked against the
Cozytouch app.

### 🔧 Only the modes your tank offers

The mode list now comes from the tank itself. On a Lineo, Prog is gone, as it
is greyed out in the app. A tank that does not report its modes keeps the
list it had.

### ⚠️ V40 setpoints are now read-only

"V40 Applied Setpoint" and "V40 Setpoint Filled By User" were sliders in °C
built on a guess. They are a charge in %, not a setting. The sliders become
unavailable and are replaced by two sensors, off by default under
Diagnostic.
