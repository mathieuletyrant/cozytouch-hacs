On an Alfea heat pump whose heating circuit is driven by a room thermostat
(a Navilink, for instance), the circuit was missing from the devices you
could add. It is now offered whatever drives it : add it with the
integration's "Add device" button and you get two read-only sensors, the
flow setpoint the heat pump is asking for and the circuit's water
temperature. Nothing is added on its own. The circuit is now called "Water
circuit", so it no longer shares the name "Heating circuit" with the room
that drives it ; if you had already added a circuit, only its displayed name
changes and its history stays. Its "Circuit Driven By Room" diagnostic
becomes "Circuit Room Count", a number, as a new entity off by default. The
flow setpoint was checked against one Alfea Excellia S's own display, not
on hardware here.
