This release gives back the temperature of rooms that had lost it, stops the
absence switch from reporting a failure that did not happen, and names the
models Atlantic added this autumn.

### 🔧 Room temperatures and absences

Some rooms behind a Navizone showed no current temperature on their
thermostat card while their sensor kept reading. Home Assistant hid it
whenever the room flagged that sensor as not valid ; the Cozytouch app's home
screen shows it anyway, and so does Home Assistant now.

On a Navizone, turning the absence on or off showed "Cozytouch did not carry
out the command" although the absence was set. It no longer does ; an
absence Cozytouch actually refuses is still an error. With the absence off,
its start and end now read unknown instead of showing the current time,
which looked like an absence nobody had programmed.

### 🌡️ Water heater power

A water heater that reports its instantaneous electrical power now gets a
power sensor in watts, enabled by default. The unit rests on one reading
from a Calypso Connecté 240 L, not checked against a meter, and Atlantic
calls the value an estimate.

### 🏷️ New models named

74 models now arrive under their name instead of `Unknown product` : Calypso
Split Trineo, Flat Evo, Lineo, Malicio 4, Vertigo, Onix Connect and Flat
WiFi water heaters, Goreli 2 towel racks, the Isilia S heat pump, and
Accessio +, Naria +, Baleares 3 and Lisbonne 3 radiators. None of them has
been seen on a live account yet. Radiators Atlantic stopped listing keep
their names.
