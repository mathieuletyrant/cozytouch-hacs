This release lets you change two things your device refused before.

### 📋 Weekly programs

A towel rail that keeps its program in the newer, extended format no longer
refuses a schedule written from Home Assistant. The `set_schedule` action and
an edit in the program calendar now write it the way the Cozytouch app does,
and the device accepts it.

### 🌡️ Heat pump setpoints

The comfort setpoint of the first room and the eco setpoint of the second are
now numbers you can set, as they are in the app. The old read-only sensor stays
in your entity list, disabled, beside the new number.
