This release is mostly about devices telling you what they are.

**Your devices have names.** 631 model ids that showed up as
`Unknown product (…)` are now named, from Atlantic's own catalogue. If a
device in Home Assistant reads as unknown, it is now worth reporting.

**Sensors have names too.** Atlantic publishes what each of its 405
capabilities means — the label, the unit, the values an enum can take — and
we now read it instead of guessing. Boilers gain their burner, flame and
pumps ; heat pumps their compressor, inputs and backup heater ; water
heaters their smart grid and heat source. Values that used to read as a bare
number now read as words.

**Atlantic Hybrellia.** Its weekly schedule, tank setpoints and overrides
were all being written to the wrong place. They work now.

**Fixes you may notice.** Room 2's comfort setpoint was labelled as
something else. The override duration is offered in the hours the app
offers, not a free number. A write you make no longer gets overwritten by
the refresh that follows it — that one affects every device.

The diagnostics dump now carries Atlantic's description of every capability
the device reports, which is what makes a device report useful.
