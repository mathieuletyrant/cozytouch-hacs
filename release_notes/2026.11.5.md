This release corrects what a room shows for the unit in front of it.

### 🔧 Air conditioners and radiators

A room with an air conditioner now reads "Heating" while it heats, instead
of "Idle". Where the unit has no room sensor, it shows no
current temperature rather than 0 °C. A room heated by a radiator no longer
offers the powerful mode or the boost preset, which belong to air
conditioners.

### 🌡️ Fan speeds

The fan and air circulation offer the speeds your unit declares, and the air
circulation select shows quiet or auto while the unit runs one.
