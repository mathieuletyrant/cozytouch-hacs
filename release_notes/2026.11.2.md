This release lets you edit a program whose days do not start at midnight.
The Cozytouch app writes such days — a Naema 2 Duo starting every day at
05:00, a room starting its Monday at 00:15 — and the schedule card, the
program calendar and `cozytouch.set_schedule` refused to save them,
showing "A day has to start at 00:00". They now save. Until a day's first
slot, the previous day's last setpoint holds, so the night between 21:00
and 05:00 is one event in the calendar rather than a gap at midnight ; and
the midnight slot can be deleted like any other, as long as a day keeps
one. The card also shows its times and temperatures in full on Android,
where it cut `05:00` to `05` and `20,5` into its degree sign, and its
default title is in your language.
